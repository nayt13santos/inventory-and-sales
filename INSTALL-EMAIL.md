**Subject:** Octogo Tracker — how to put it on your phone (5 minutes)

Hi,

Here's how to get the Octogo app onto your phone. It takes about five minutes and it's free — no app store, no account to create.

**Link:** https://nayt13santos.github.io/octogo-tracker/

I'll send you the **access code** in a separate message. Don't worry about it until Step 3.

---

## Step 1 — Open the link on your phone

Tap the link above **on the phone you'll be using**.

- **Android:** open it in **Chrome**
- **iPhone:** open it in **Safari**

This matters — on iPhone it won't install from Chrome.

You'll see the app with an orange banner at the top saying it's in demo mode. That's normal; we fix it in Step 3.

---

## Step 2 — Add it to your home screen

**On Android (Chrome):**
1. Tap the **⋮** menu (three dots, top right)
2. Tap **Add to Home screen**
3. Tap **Install** (or **Add**)

**On iPhone (Safari):**
1. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom)
2. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
3. Tap **Add** (top right)

You should now have an **Octogo** icon on your home screen — the octopus with the takoyaki. Open the app from that icon from now on, not from the browser.

---

## Step 3 — Connect it (do this once)

Open the app from your new home screen icon, then:

1. Tap **More** (bottom right)
2. Under **API setup**, fill in the two boxes:
   - **Apps Script Web App URL** — paste the long link I sent you (it ends in `/exec`)
   - **Token (from the Settings tab of the sheet)** — paste the access code I sent you
3. Tap **Test connection**

If it says **Connected**, you're done. The orange demo banner will disappear.

If it doesn't connect, check that you pasted both without extra spaces — it's easy to miss a character. Then try again.

---

## Step 4 — That's it. Here's how you use it each night

Open the app when you close up, tap **Sales**, and work down the screen:

1. **Container counts** — how many boxes you started the day with, and how many are left. The app works out how many sold.
2. **How were they paid** — tap the **+** for the cheese ones, and for anything paid by **GCash**. Everything else is plain cash and you don't type it.
3. **Custom order** — if someone ordered specially, put the amount here.
4. **Wage for this day** — already filled in. Change it only for a half day.
5. **Stock used today** — tap **+** once for each whole thing you opened: one gallon of sauce, one pack of flour.
6. Tap **Save day**.

Log any spending under the **Expenses** tab as it happens.

---

## Good to know

**It works without signal.** The stall doesn't need internet. Enter everything as normal — it saves on the phone and sends itself the next time you have a connection. The little tag at the top tells you: **Synced** means everything's in, a number means it's still waiting to send.

**Don't worry about mistakes.** You can open any earlier day (tap the date at the top) and fix it. Nothing is locked.

**If the app ever says something is "not saved"**, it'll tell you which day and what went wrong, with a button to try again. Nothing is lost when that happens.

**The app updates itself.** You'll never need to reinstall it.
